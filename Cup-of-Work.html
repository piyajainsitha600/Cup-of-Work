Get to know us

Who Are We?

Cup of Work is a digital services brand built around one simple idea: good work starts with understanding what a project actually needs.

We help businesses, entrepreneurs, and content creators with practical digital tasks across research, data management, writing, design, and video editing. Whether you need information organized into a clear spreadsheet, written content refined, a visual asset created, or a video edited, our goal is to deliver work that fits your brief and helps you move forward.

We believe in clear communication, realistic deadlines, attention to detail, and a straightforward project-based approach. Every project begins with understanding your requirements, agreeing on the deliverables, and working toward a result that meets your needs.

Cup of Work is designed to be flexible: you bring the idea or challenge, and we work with you to find a practical solution.

“Your project deserves attention to detail—not a one-size-fits-all solution.”

Frequently Asked Questions

What services do you provide?

We offer selected project-based services across research, spreadsheets, data organization, writing, editing, design, video editing, and digital project support.

How do I get a price?

Pricing depends on the project scope, complexity, deliverables, and deadline. Contact us with your requirements to request a quote.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. Cup of Work welcomes project enquiries from international clients, subject to project suitability and payment arrangements.

How long does a project take?

Timelines depend on the size and complexity of the project. We discuss an estimated delivery date before work begins.

Can I request a custom service?

Yes. If your request is related to our digital support capabilities, send us the details and we can discuss whether it is a suitable fit.

Do you offer revisions?

Revision arrangements are agreed before the project begins. The agreed scope and number of included revisions will be confirmed in the project discussion.
